About Progylm

A learning institute built on one belief: understanding before everything.

Progylm exists to give NEET-UG and CUET-UG aspirants access to Biology and Physics teaching that is rigorous, concept-first, and completely free. Not a shortcut. Not a crash course. A genuine attempt to make science understandable at its roots.

What Progylm is

Free education, built to last.

Progylm is a YouTube-first learning institute. Every lesson in Biology and Physics is published free — no fees, no locked content, no paid tiers. The teaching is designed not just to help you pass an exam, but to build the kind of understanding that makes you a stronger science student long after the result is out.

The focus is NEET-UG and CUET-UG, but the approach is deeper than any single exam. When concepts are understood properly, they transfer — to boards, to entrance tests, to every question framed in a way you haven't seen before.

Why free

Trust is built before anything else.

Progylm starts with free education because a strong learning relationship should not begin with a transaction. Students who are serious about NEET-UG and CUET-UG deserve access to serious teaching — and that access should not depend on their ability to pay for it.

The philosophy behind the institute

Aspire. Condition. Transform.

This tagline is not just an identity — it is the learning process Progylm is built around.

Aspire

Every student needs a goal that is worth their best effort. Aspiration is what drives the hours, the revision, and the discipline that serious preparation demands. Progylm begins by taking your goal seriously.

Condition

Success in competitive exams is not just about what you know — it is about how your mind is trained to think under pressure. Conditioning means building the mental frameworks, structured study habits, and conceptual clarity that make the right answer feel obvious, not guessed.

Transform

The goal is not just a score. It is a student who thinks differently — who approaches Biology and Physics with genuine confidence because the foundation has been built right. That transformation happens one well-understood concept at a time.

Exam direction

Shaped entirely around NEET-UG and CUET-UG.

Every lesson, every note, every strategy at Progylm is built with exam intent. The content is mapped to what NEET-UG and CUET-UG actually test — not what sounds impressive on a syllabus. Understanding the chapter is the goal. Performing on exam day is the outcome.

What this institute stands for

The values Progylm will not compromise on.

  • Honest teaching without inflated promises or fear-based messaging.
  • Conceptual depth before shortcuts — understanding before memorisation.
  • NCERT as the primary text, treated with the seriousness it deserves.
  • Respect for every student who is still building their confidence.
  • A structured, professional learning environment — free, always.
