FAQ

Before you begin.

Is Progylm completely free?

Yes — entirely. All Biology and Physics lessons for NEET-UG and CUET-UG are published free on YouTube. There are no fees, no paid tiers, and no locked content. Free is not a temporary offer — it is the model.

Who is Progylm for?

Primarily for NEET-UG and CUET-UG aspirants studying Biology and Physics. School students building strong science foundations will also benefit. If you want to understand the subject — not just memorise it — Progylm is built for you.

What makes Progylm different from other YouTube channels?

The teaching here is structured around six core pillars: concept-first learning, NCERT mastery, smart notes and flowcharts, scientifically grounded memory techniques, long-term retention strategies, and exam-oriented application. Most channels teach content. Progylm teaches you how to think about the content.

How should I start?

Go to the Progylm YouTube channel and begin with whichever subject or chapter feels most challenging. Watch with the intent to understand — not just to note down. If something is unclear, revisit it. Clarity takes priority over speed here.

Does Progylm cover both NEET-UG and CUET-UG?

Yes. The Biology and Physics content is designed to serve both NEET-UG and CUET-UG aspirants. The syllabus overlap between the two exams is significant, and the concept-first approach works for both.
